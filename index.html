Where did it go?

FinSync keeps your expenses, debts and EMIs, investments and savings goals in one place. Everything is synced to your Google account. It's free and has no ads.

Android 7.0 or later · 4 MBDownload APK
iPhone · in SafariOpen web app

Your month at a glance

See your net balance, spending rate, where the money went, and the EMIs due this month.

Bank SMS to expenses

On Android, FinSync turns bank alerts into draft expenses. You review each one and tap to add it.

Debts and EMIs

Track what you owe, what you're owed, and every loan installment.

Investments and goals

Follow your portfolio's profit and loss, and see how much to save each month to hit a goal.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download APK.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. Choose Continue with Google.

Add to iPhone

  1. Open Open web app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open FinSync from your home screen and sign in with Google.
